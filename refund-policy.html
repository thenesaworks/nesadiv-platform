Cancellation & Refund Policy

Applies to all digital reports and subscriptions purchased on NesaDiv (www.nesadiv.com) via Razorpay

1. Digital Report Purchases (Pro Se PSWR, Premium PSWR & AWR)

Because NesaDiv salary dossiers (including the ₹299 Pro Se PSWR, Premium PSWR, and Appraisal Worthiness Report) are custom-computed and delivered digitally immediately upon payment confirmation, orders cannot be cancelled or refunded once the report has been generated and delivered to your email or Member Dashboard.

2. Eligible Refund Scenarios

We issue a 100% full refund under any of the following conditions:

  • Duplicate Payment: Your card, UPI, or bank account was accidentally charged more than once for the same order.
  • Failed Delivery / Technical Error: Payment was successfully debited, but due to a technical error on our platform, your report was not generated or delivered within 24 hours.
  • Unused Sponsored Vouchers: A sponsored/gifted report voucher that has not been redeemed by the recipient within 7 days of purchase, upon request by the original purchaser.

3. Employer Suite Subscriptions

Employer quarterly plans (Growth Starter and Scale & GCC) can be cancelled at any time before the next billing cycle by emailing enterprise@nesadiv.com. Access remains active until the end of the current paid quarter. Partial-quarter refunds are not provided once workspace access and live salary bands have been unlocked.

4. How to Request a Refund & Processing Timeline

To request an eligible refund, email support@nesadiv.com within 7 days of the transaction with your Razorpay Payment ID and registered email address. Approved refunds are initiated back to your original payment method within 2 business days and typically reflect in your account within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or UPI provider.
