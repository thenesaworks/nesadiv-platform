The NesaDiv Forensic Methodology

Most Indian salary portals rely on unverified, self-reported headline CTCs that mix guaranteed base pay with illiquid ESOPs and inflated bonuses. NesaDiv uses a deterministic, four-stage framework to isolate real cash value.

01

Fixed Gross Isolation (Stripping Phantom CTC)

Before benchmarking any offer or current salary, our engine strips away all non-monthly-cash and non-guaranteed components—collectively termed Phantom CTC:

Variable & Performance Pay Discretionary or company-linked bonuses that are not guaranteed in monthly payroll.
ESOPs, RSUs & Retention Bonuses Multi-year vesting schedules, illiquid private equity, and one-time joining payouts.
Employer PF & Gratuity Statutory retiral contributions locked behind 5-year service rules or EPFO withdrawals.
Corporate Insurance & Perks Group health premiums and notional office benefits bundled into headline CTC.
02

Deterministic 2026–27 New Tax Regime Engine

NesaDiv never uses AI to guess tax numbers. All monthly take-home calculations run through deterministic, rule-based arithmetic aligned with India's Tax Year 2026–27 New Tax Regime:

  • ₹75,000 Standard Deduction applied automatically to Fixed Gross salary.
  • Section 87A Rebate & Marginal Relief: Nil income tax up to ₹12,00,000 taxable income, with exact marginal relief above the threshold.
  • Statutory Surcharge & 4% Cess: Progressive New Regime slabs (5% to 30%), surcharge capped at 25%, plus 4% Health & Education Cess.
  • Employee Deductions: Deducts Employee PF and applicable state Professional Tax to arrive at exact True Net Monthly In-Hand.
