Privacy Policy

Aligned with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act · Strict Zero-BGV Policy

1. Our Zero-BGV Guarantee

NesaDiv Compensation Intelligence ("NesaDiv", "we", "our") operates strictly as a compensation benchmarking and forensic CTC analysis platform. We never contact your current or former employers, never conduct background verification (BGV), and never sell or disclose your personal identity or compensation inputs to recruiters, agencies, or third-party data brokers.

2. Information We Collect

  • Free Tools (CTC Decompiler): Calculations run entirely in your browser. We do not store your CTC inputs on our servers.
  • Pro Se PSWR (₹299 No-Login Reports): We collect your name, delivery email address, role, experience band, city, employer tier, and fixed salary strictly to generate and email your private report.
  • Member & Employer Dashboards: We collect your account email, name, and saved report parameters via encrypted Supabase authentication.
  • Payment Processing: All payments are processed securely by Razorpay. NesaDiv never stores your credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or banking credentials.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your inputs are used exclusively to compute your requested salary dossiers, deliver private report links via email, maintain your authenticated dashboard history, and improve anonymized, aggregated market percentile models where no individual can ever be identified.

4. Data Security & Retention

All data in transit is protected via TLS/HTTPS encryption. Authenticated records are protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring only you can access your saved dossiers. We retain report records only as long as necessary to provide your access or comply with Indian accounting and tax regulations.

5. Your Rights Under Indian Data Protection Law

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your personal data stored with NesaDiv. To exercise these rights, email our Data Protection Desk at support@nesadiv.com from your registered email address.
