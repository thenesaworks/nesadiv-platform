What is the difference between the Pro Se PSWR (₹299) and the Member Dashboard Premium PSWR? +

The Pro Se PSWR (₹299) is a zero-login, email-delivered Personal Salary Worthiness Report designed for fast HR screening calls and quick market checks. The Member Dashboard Premium PSWR and Appraisal Worthiness Report (AWR) are authenticated, multi-tier dossiers comparing IT Services, Startups, and GCCs alongside ESOP modeling and saved history.

Who is NesaDiv built for? +

NesaDiv is built exclusively for experienced Indian professionals with 6+ months of work experience and corporate HR/Talent teams. Freshers with under 6 months of experience are served by our sister platform, Competence.co.in.

Does NesaDiv ever contact my current or former employer? +

Never. We operate under a strict Zero-BGV Policy. Your compensation inputs are used strictly to compute your report and are never shared with your current employer, past employers, or third-party recruiters.

How is True Net Monthly In-Hand calculated? +

Our Forensic CTC Decompiler strips out non-monthly-cash items (variable bonus, ESOPs, joining bonus, employer PF, gratuity, and insurance) to isolate Fixed Gross Salary, then applies India's deterministic FY 2026–27 New Tax Regime slabs, ₹75,000 standard deduction, Section 87A rebate, surcharge, 4% cess, employee PF, and professional tax.

How quickly are reports delivered after payment? +

All reports are delivered digitally and instantaneously. As soon as your payment clears, your print-ready PDF dossier unlocks on-screen and is dispatched to your email address.
